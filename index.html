Biography

Clinical care at the bedside, leadership across the specialty.

Prof Carson's career has spanned the paediatric intensive care unit at Temple Street and the leadership of anaesthesiology in Ireland.

Prof Kevin Carson is a consultant in Intensive Care Medicine at Children's Health Ireland (CHI) at Temple Street, part of CHI's national Critical Care Medicine service. At Temple Street he has been Director of the Department of Anaesthesia, Intensive Care and Pain Medicine and Clinical Director of the Paediatric Intensive Care Unit. He has a particular interest in chronic pain in children.

He qualified in medicine (MB BS) at the University of London in 1985. He became a Fellow of the Faculty of Anaesthetists, RCSI, in 1992 and joined the Specialist Register in Anaesthesia in 1997. He is Professor of Paediatric Anaesthesia on the UCD clinical pathway, and Supervisor of Training at Temple Street for the Joint Faculty of Intensive Care Medicine of Ireland.

From 2015 to 2018 he was President of the College of Anaesthesiologists of Ireland, leading the College through its 20th anniversary as an independent college.

Leadership

President, College of Anaesthesiologists of Ireland

A three-year term that ended in September 2018, the year the College marked 20 years as an independent college.

CAI · 2015 – 2018

Modernising a national specialty

During his presidency the College renamed the specialty “anaesthesiology”, expanded international training partnerships, invested in research and simulation, and made doctors' wellbeing a national priority.

It also awarded its highest honour, an Honorary Fellowship, to President Michael D. Higgins.
